# Ambulando Remote — draft store listing

Control Ambulando voice messages from your Vivoactive 6. Record with a Bluetooth microphone connected to your Android phone; this app does not record from the watch.

Choose Start, wait for the phone to confirm recording, then Finish to send to your explicitly selected Ambulando conversation. Cancel discards the current unsent recording. Status and Retry help recover interrupted communication and upload failures.

Requires Ambulando for Android, Garmin Connect, a paired Vivoactive 6, an unlocked signed-in Ambulando account and a Bluetooth headset with microphone support. Configure the destination in Ambulando first. Phone confirmation is required unless optional Android companion screen-opening access is configured and works on your device. The phone may light up and show the recording screen. Background availability depends on Android keeping Ambulando's process alive. Music pause depends on the player respecting audio focus; music is not resumed automatically.

Privacy: the watch exchanges control/status messages only. Audio is recorded on the phone and sent through your configured Ambulando account to the conversation you select. Failed uploads retain audio on the phone for retry. Garmin Connect handles watch/phone transport. No watch microphone, independent watch upload or guaranteed background wake-up is claimed.
